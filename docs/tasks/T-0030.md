## Goal
The user asks questions about their mail ("这周有哪些作业要交", "Canvas 上次说了什么"). The Hub
answers from stored `ok` summaries through `models.service`, so the budget cap, audit and key
handling are kept. The answer lists the mails it used. Design: architect, 2026-11-11.

## Constraints
- Stateless: the conversation lives in the browser, and the last 6 turns are sent with each
  question. No new table. The question text is never stored (`model_call.ref = ""`).
- Only `ok` summaries are sent, and only fresh ones: if a mail changed after its summary,
  the summary is not used. Sensitive, unreadable, pending and failed mail are never sent,
  not even the subject. Mail bodies are never sent.
- Retrieval needs no new dependency:
  - tokens are ASCII words plus CJK bigrams, scored by IDF;
  - the 20 newest mails are always included, plus up to 10 with deadlines from yesterday to
    14 days ahead;
  - at most 60 lines.
- Mail-controlled text is defanged: delimiter tags become `[tag]`, and newlines are
  flattened. The prompt says that mail text is data, and forbids doing homework (CIS rule).
- Citations are mapped from short refs (m1..m60) back to ids. Refs that were not supplied are
  dropped. At most 8 are kept.
- Errors: `ModelUnavailable` → 503 with a Chinese notice and the header
  `X-Model-Unavailable`; `ModelFailed` → 502.
- Batch rules are not part of this card (they need a table and the user's sign-off).
