## Goal
The web side of ADR 0034, on 邮件:
- a panel shows 「未处理 N 封」, a 帮我整理 toggle, and one card per group: label, count,
  the first 3 AI titles, a hint, 「全部标为已处理（N）」, and 「以后也这样（…）」 when the
  group offers a rule;
- 撤销 undoes the last batch;
- rules have on/off switches;
- the list gets a 未处理 filter, 需处理 leaves out handled mail, lines show 已处理 (or
  已处理·规则), and an opened mail has 标为已处理 / 改回未处理.
