## Goal
The user (2026-11-26) wants the EarthPulse-style changelog in Personal OS: hovering the
top-left logo shows the newest version (number, date, what changed) and "点击看全部版本记录";
a click opens every version, the newest marked 当前. History back to 2026-11-10 is written
in plain Chinese from the merged cards. CLAUDE.md makes every user-visible change add a line.
The user also asked (same day) for the logo to be their original image, not T-0074's
redrawn SVG: the PNG is cropped from their file, and the redrawn SVG is removed.
