## Goal
ADR 0037, part 2. `GET /gpa` and a 成绩 page with one card per credit course (non-credit
shells are left out). Each card shows:
- Canvas's current score and letter;
- per-group weight, percent and graded count;
- completion: done/due, missing, late;
- missing work, work due in the next 14 days, and recent grades;
- what the ungraded rest needs on average for A/B/C (lines are estimates);
- course materials by module.

The GPA estimate averages grade points with equal units, and says so.

Also fixed: the Canvas hub verify counted every item and broke once T-0040 added
courses. It now counts work items, plus the courses and materials separately.
