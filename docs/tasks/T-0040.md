## Goal
Collect the raw facts the GPA module (T-0041) needs, into `synced_item` (no schema change):
- `canvas_course`: Canvas's current score and grade, its "final" score (which counts
  ungraded work as 0), whether groups are weighted, and the groups with their weights;
- `canvas_assignment` gains group id, letter grade, graded time, excused, and
  omitted-from-final;
- `canvas_material`: files, pages and links listed in modules (name, module, kind, Canvas
  link). Nothing is downloaded.

A 404 on groups or modules means "none". A 401/403 means unknown: the course is skipped
for that resource and nothing is pruned.
