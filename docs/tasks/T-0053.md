## Goal
ADR 0041 linked graded work to sections by the section numbers in Canvas titles. ECON
(and ENGL) do not number their work: the homework is "Homework - Monetary Policy" and the
notes are "Monetary Policy.doc". A section may now carry title patterns (`match`, the same
whole-title patterns as grade categories: `*`, `#`, at most three wildcards) that pick its
assignments and module items. Numbered courses are unchanged.

The ECON map itself is data, written by the assistant from the teacher's Canvas material
and imported with `scripts/academic/import_map.py`; the file stays in `D:\dev-cache`.

## Acceptance (runnable)
- `uv run pytest tests/hub/test_course_map.py -q`:
  - a section with patterns gets its homework (stray spaces in the Canvas title ignored)
    and its module items; a shorter title does not take a longer chapter's work;
  - mastery and the exam map's order follow from that work; the exam is not practice;
  - more than three wildcards, an empty pattern, or patterns on a chapter give 422.
- On the live Hub after the update, with the ECON map imported: every chapter taught so
  far shows its homework and the teacher's files; Exam 2's map lists the study guide
  (local screenshots, `.evidence/T-0053/`).
- `uv run python -m tools.check` exits 0.
